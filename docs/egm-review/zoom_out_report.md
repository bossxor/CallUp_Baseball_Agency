# Initial Zoom Out

- **Major — milestone order.** Prove a complete Android agency loop with weekly persistence before requiring four simulated markets. The implementation document makes persistence foundational (§2) but puts restore in milestone 4 (§5). Keep the four-market goal explicit.
- **Major — ownership and registration.** The clock, roster, offers, inbox and accounting need one commit boundary. At acceptance, reserve the slot and budget. Define when the claim is used and how failed registration releases it without a fee or affiliation change (§§2–3).
- **Major — interruption ownership.** Use the weekly event classification to decide both what the player sees and when batched advance stops. An important update can have no pending action (§2; game design §§2, 7).
- **Minor — Android evidence.** Demonstrate offline startup, Android Back, restart and reduced motion with the first playable slice (§§4–6).

The case for keeping the larger goal is that independent markets, recoverable reports and one-time fees define the intended agency simulation. The findings change sequencing and decision ownership.
